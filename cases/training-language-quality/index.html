RISTO-LAB

From formal language to practical instructions

Preserve the procedure while making four actions clear to a new employee.

Synthetic evaluation example / AI-assisted synthetic material. Not a new live GPT test or an independent human evaluation.

01 Problem02 Input & reference03 Process04 Output05 Quality check06 Evidence & limits

Problem

Formal, grammatical instructions can be difficult to use and can omit the objects an employee must actually check.

Input & reference

For a new employee with professional Italian B1, produce at most four points and 45 words, with one main action per point. Preserve the escalation and final-check condition.

Original Italian evidence

Original brief

Checklist in italiano per nuovo collaboratore B1; massimo 4 punti e 45 parole; una azione principale per punto.

Reference facts

Prima del servizio controllare bicchieri e tovaglioli. Se manca materiale avvisare il responsabile di turno. Iniziare solo dopo il controllo finale del responsabile.

Occorre ottemperare alla verifica preliminare delle dotazioni di servizio, procedendo alla segnalazione delle eventuali carenze al preposto di turno. L'avvio delle attività deve essere subordinato alla validazione conclusiva delle disponibilità operative da parte del medesimo referente.

Process

  1. Identify the concrete objects, the missing-material condition and the responsible person.
  2. Replace abstract wording with direct actions without adding new obligations.
  3. Check format, length and preserved conditions; reserve comprehension claims for a human test.

Output

A four-action checklist explicitly names glasses and napkins, tells the employee whom to notify and keeps service conditional on the supervisor’s final check.

1. Controlla i bicchieri. 2. Controlla i tovaglioli. 3. Se manca materiale, avvisa il responsabile di turno. 4. Inizia il servizio solo dopo il controllo finale del responsabile.

PASS / Quattro punti, oggetti e condizioni espliciti; comprensione B1 non testata con persone.

Quality check

Documentary check
The canonical script checks four numbered lines, the 45-word limit and the required objects/condition. Documentary review assigns FAIL to A and PASS to B.
Human check pending
No B1 participant responses have been collected. A proposed test asks what to check, whom to notify and when service may start. Readability is not a measured learning outcome.

What this demonstrates

  • Audience awareness
  • Checklists
  • Training design

Evidence & limits

The report is pre-Builder. The 50 scenarios are prepared test cases, not 50 completed live tests. B1 comprehension has not been tested with participants.
