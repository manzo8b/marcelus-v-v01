Free resource, v1.0 — English

1. GOAL
What result do I want? _______
Who will use it? _______
In which situation? _______

2. INPUTS AND REFERENCES
What facts are already known? _______
Which documents can be used? _______
Which facts are missing or uncertain? _______

3. REQUIREMENTS
Language, tone and audience: _______
Expected format: _______
Mandatory constraints: _______
Content to exclude: _______

4. RELIABILITY
Which claims need a source or check? _______
What must not be invented? _______
Privacy, copyright or safety concerns? _______

5. ACCEPTANCE CHECK
[ ] Addresses the goal.
[ ] Follows the constraints.
[ ] Distinguishes facts and guesses.
[ ] Adds no unsupported facts.
[ ] Is understandable without specialist jargon.

6. TEMPLATE PROMPT
"I need [RESULT] for [AUDIENCE] in [CONTEXT].
Use [INPUTS AND REFERENCES]. Follow [CONSTRAINTS].
Write in [LANGUAGE] and organize as [FORMAT].
Do not invent missing information; explain what cannot be verified.
Check the result against [ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA]."

7. REVIEW
What worked? _______
What was misunderstood? _______
What should I improve next? _______

NOTE
Clear instructions make review easier but do not guarantee accuracy. Do not submit confidential information without authorization.
