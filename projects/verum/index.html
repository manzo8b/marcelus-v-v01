VERUM technique

VERUM Dual-Layer Review

What a text claims and how it frames the claim are different questions. Read the evidence and the language separately, then check that a rewrite has preserved the meaning.

A calmer tone does not add evidence. A softer rewrite can still change the claim.

Supplied wording · English

Something big is happening in AI right now.

Layer 1 / Claims and evidence

AI is changing in a significant way, but neither “big” nor “right now” has a defined measure or interval.

Evidence needed

A dated period, a defined capability/adoption indicator and relevant source material.

Layer 2 / Language and framing

“big” amplifies magnitude without specifying it. This can suggest importance, but does not establish a measured persuasive effect.

Rewrite supplied in the example

Recent developments in AI suggest notable changes in the field.

Meaning-preservation check

“suggest” qualifies the assertion, but “notable changes” still supplies no magnitude indicator. A more restrained tone has not made the claim externally verifiable.

Attributed wording, not an endorsement

The source describes current AI developments as significant, without defining their magnitude or timeframe.

Supplied wording · English

Most people don’t realize the scale of this shift.

Layer 1 / Claims and evidence

A majority is said to lack awareness of a change. Both the population and the scale of that change need definition.

Evidence needed

A defined population, survey question, sample, date and measure of the change.

Layer 2 / Language and framing

“Most people” and “don’t realize” create an awareness contrast. An informed/less-informed reading is plausible, not proof of motive or actual audience response.

Rewrite supplied in the example

Some observers may not yet be aware of the potential scale of these developments.

Meaning-preservation check

“Most people” becomes “Some observers”: both the quantity and the group change. Under the stated preservation task, this is substantive drift even though the wording sounds more cautious.

Attributed wording, not an endorsement

The source claims that most people do not recognize the scale of the shift; no supporting population survey is supplied.

Supplied wording · English

This changes everything.

Layer 1 / Claims and evidence

An unspecified change is presented as universal. The referent, affected domains and time horizon are undefined.

Evidence needed

Defined affected domains, a time horizon and observable outcomes.

Layer 2 / Language and framing

“everything” gives the sentence totalizing scope. That is an observable wording choice, not proof that the author intends to manipulate anyone.

Rewrite supplied in the example

These developments could have broad implications across multiple sectors.

Meaning-preservation check

“everything” becomes “broad implications across multiple sectors”. This narrows the scope. It may be sensible editing under another task, but does not preserve the original universal quantifier.

Attributed wording, not an endorsement

The source describes the change as affecting everything, without specifying the affected domains or time horizon.

Task boundary

This editorial comparison asks for less emphatic wording while preserving populations and quantifiers. If a task instead permits changing scope, the rewrite is not automatically a failure. The original generation prompt is unavailable.

What this example establishes

An AI-assisted editorial analysis of owner-supplied material, not a live model run or a measured reader study. Linguistic interpretations are not proof of author intent or factual falsity. No numerical bias reduction or inter-annotator agreement is claimed. The original publication and author of the excerpts have not been verified.

Original Italian evidence

Original brief

Scrivi un breve testo informativo in italiano usando soltanto i fatti forniti.

Reference facts

Centro culturale aperto martedì-domenica 09:00-19:00; chiuso lunedì; biglietto ordinario 12 euro; minori di 18 anni gratis; ultimo ingresso 18:00.

Candidate / variant

FAIL
Il centro culturale è aperto tutti i giorni dalle 9:00 alle 19:00. Il biglietto costa 15 euro, mentre i bambini sotto i 12 anni entrano gratuitamente. L'ultimo ingresso è consentito alle 18:30. Ti consigliamo di arrivare presto per evitare lunghe code.

Apertura, prezzo, fascia gratuita e ultimo ingresso alterati; code non documentate.

Reference-preserving variant

PASS
Il centro culturale è aperto da martedì a domenica dalle 09:00 alle 19:00 ed è chiuso il lunedì. Il biglietto ordinario costa 12 euro; l'ingresso è gratuito per i minori di 18 anni. L'ultimo ingresso è alle 18:00.

I cinque fatti sono preservati.

Four factual attributes change: opening days, price, the free-entry age and last entry. The queue prediction is also unsupported. The corrected version preserves all five reference facts.
