INDEPENDENT EDITORIAL PROJECT

S-COMODO

A separate editorial project about current events and their historical context. Its purpose is to make the editorial process — and the limits of AI assistance — visible.

In preparation

What it explores

Public events, historical comparisons and sources that help readers understand the context of a story.

How AI is used

AI may assist with research organization and early drafts. Editorial direction, fact checking, corrections and the decision to publish require human review.

Current status

Editorial drafts are being prepared. This page is an introduction, not evidence of published articles or a launched magazine.

This project has its own editorial identity. Its articles, when approved and published, will not represent the position of MARCELUS AI LAB.
