Illustrative post / EU 2024

A real number, the wrong conclusion

In 2024, renewables supplied 47.5% of EU gross electricity consumption. So 47.5% of all EU energy, including transport and heating, was renewable. The electricity share rose by 2.1% in relative terms from 2023. This proves that every household’s bill fell by 30% because of renewables.
